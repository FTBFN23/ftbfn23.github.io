❓ How Does It Work?

In the WoW Forever beta, a system bug stops your addons from writing their data to the SavedVariables files on your computer. ForeverSVFix works by temporarily modifying how the game reads those files, so your addon data is written correctly. It’s like a quick band-aid that restores normal saving behavior. You don’t need to know anything about files, folders, or code—the tool does it all for you.
